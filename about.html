About us

Illustration by the Anime Haven team.

Hola! I'm Sofia Martinez, and I opened Anime Haven in 2019 so Dublin had somewhere to talk about manga for hours.

What started as a few shelves of my own favourites is now a shop with over 1,200 titles, plus figures, Blu-rays and merch. But the best part is still the people who come in to chat about what they're reading.

"The books I love most are the ones I push into people's hands."

What you'll find here

  • Manga, restocked every Thursday
  • Figures, from scale figures to blind boxes
  • Blu-ray box sets and films
  • Merch: totes, pins and stationery
  • Free events: watch nights, drawing club and cosplay contests
